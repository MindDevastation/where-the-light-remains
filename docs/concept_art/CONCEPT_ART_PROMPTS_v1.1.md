# CONCEPT_ART_PROMPTS v1.1 — canonical production correction

Use the shared style language: stylized realistic 3D game concept art, romantic first-person puzzle adventure, elegant magical observatory/archive, readable silhouettes, modular production-friendly architecture, aged brass, dark walnut, pale/warm stone, clear/frosted glass, restrained vegetation, moonlit blue + warm archive gold + subtle dawn rose. Avoid gothic horror, sci-fi neon, clutter and photoreal excess.

**Canon rule:** gameplay mechanics below are authoritative. Never replace them with generic fantasy devices merely because an image looks attractive.

## S00 Prologue
Exterior night approach to the same observatory later seen at dawn. Clear readable path, closed main door, dormant mechanisms, almost no active magical light except the first fragile spark. No horror framing.

## S01 Central Observatory
Circular hub with central Archive mechanism/core orrery, five readable wing gates and floor/wall light channels. Starting lens/socket must be visually understandable. Central mechanism is the primary hero prop and must support asleep/awakened/final states.

## S02 Warmth / Light
Show the exact puzzle language: **three concentric optical rings**, a light emitter/star target, **five-position focus wheel**, and Hearth activation. The room transitions from cool optical precision to warm human-scale light. Do not substitute generic lantern puzzles.

## S03 Life / Voice
Show **central waveform projector**, three local resonators with three discrete states, short/medium/long sample logic, three impulse rings and a final sprout/life reveal. Sound logic must have visible equivalents. Avoid literal heartbeat or whisper imagery.

## S04 First Meeting
Stylized raid-memory diorama with **two-rune cooperative stabilization**. Two characters participate in the same ordinary raid moment; no combat mechanic, destiny framing, heroic pose or explicit romance. Background participants may be echo silhouettes.

## S05 Laughter / Lightness
Hero puzzle is a **kinetic mobile** with three counterweights, brake and stoppers; Feather/Bells are the narrative reward language. Airy/playful but elegant, never cartoonish. Mechanism should read clearly from first-person distance.

## S06 Egg
Linear readable sequence: stealth path toward the egg → pickup → boss wakes → clear chase route with collapsing ceiling/debris, poison lanes and snake warnings → ledge/rescue. Tension is comic/affectionate rather than deadly. No health-bar/death-game visual language. Rescue must remain scripted, not physics-chaotic.

## S07 Seriousness / Smile
Quiet reflective room with **frosted memory glass**, replay shutter and **four symbol discs × three states**, plus a Double Moon device with discrete orientations. Design should preserve negative space and calm observation. Do not replace with generic mirror alignment.

## S08 Sincerity / Admiration
Show the exact **constellation table**, four observers/sectors, eight star nodes/connecting lines, main crystal assembly, three filter frames × three states and frosted projection target. Include Future Record panel with `Не создана` as a production beat. Beautiful but not grander than finale.

## S09 What Hasn't Happened Yet
A **possible future meeting**, never a promise or mutually decided future. Begin in an Archive/fantasy path and gradually dissolve into a neutral real-world bridge/path/park analogue with water/air/ordinary lighting. Two figures may approach/progress in parallel, but composition must remain open and unresolved. No wedding/date-promise symbolism, no guaranteed reunion, no possessive framing.

## S10 Return
Reuse the exact Central Observatory architecture. Fully restored state; five wing channels converge; central mechanism unfolds final table and presents ten sigils in found/free arrangement.

## S11 Order of Light
Five pair nodes, ten placement sockets, readable swap states, continuous final light path. No hidden letters visible yet. Puzzle must remain legible in first person.

## S12 Poem
Ten readable poem blocks merge into a calm full-poem layout. Typography clarity over spectacle.

## S13 Acrostic
Initial letters remain after poem dissolve and rearrange into the hidden phrase. Magical but restrained; no extra symbols that reveal the answer earlier.

## S14 Confession
`Я люблю тебя` in the restored Archive. Calm/released light, intimate scale, no character-reaction shot, no response buttons and no visual pressure.

## S15 Dawn
Same observatory/exterior as Prologue, transformed only by restored state and dawn. Camera exits the same main door into a calm wide shot. **Do not show the heroine accepting/rejecting/responding.** Emotional closure belongs to light, architecture and atmosphere — not a depicted reciprocal outcome.

## Character references
Generate original stylized interpretations from supplied screenshots. Preserve only broad silhouette/color/personality cues needed for recognition. Do not copy proprietary logos, exact armor geometry or extracted game assets. No facial-animation requirement.

## Hero prop requirements
Central mechanism, five wing mechanisms, egg, sigil system and final table must each have readable silhouette, material contrast and emissive/lighting states that communicate interaction within ~2–3 seconds. Reuse the Archive material/modular language rather than inventing unrelated style families.

## Production sheets
Maintain dedicated material, architecture shape-language and lighting sheets. They are cross-stage references and should favor reusable families over unique decorative assets.
